# Agent Rules & Safety Guidelines

## ⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY RULES FOR CODING AGENTS

### 1. Protection of Paratext Projects & Installation Folders
- **Strictly DO NOT modify, overwrite, move, or delete** any files or folders in **My Paratext 9 Projects** (regardless of drive or location, e.g., `C:\My Paratext 9 Projects`, `D:\My Paratext 9 Projects`, etc.).
- **Strictly DO NOT modify, overwrite, or alter** any files in the **Paratext 9 Installation folder** (e.g., `C:\Program Files\Paratext 9`, `C:\Program Files (x86)\Paratext 9`, or any installation path).
- All Paratext program directories and user project directories are strictly off-limits for automated file operations.

### 2. Explicit Permission Required for Scripture / USFM Changes
- You must **ask for explicit user permission** before making or proposing any change to any Scripture or USFM files (`.usfm`, `.sfm`, scripture databases, or text files containing biblical text).
- Always show the target file, verse/chapter references, and exact proposed diff, and wait for user confirmation prior to taking any action.

### 3. No Autonomous Scripture Editing
- **You are NOT allowed to edit any scriptures on your own.**
- Never perform autonomous, background, batch, or unattended edits to scripture content, markers, or translation data.
- All scripture modifications must remain strictly under the direct agency, review, and approval of the human translator/user.
